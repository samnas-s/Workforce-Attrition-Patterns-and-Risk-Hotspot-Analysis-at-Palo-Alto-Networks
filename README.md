# Workforce-Attrition-Patterns-and-Risk-Hotspot-Analysis-at-Palo-Alto-Networks (created using power bi)
## project objectives
Analyze overall employee attrition.
Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
Analyze attrition across different age and tenure groups.
Examine the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.
Analyze the impact of business travel on attrition.
Compare early-career and experienced employees.
Identify potential HR risk hotspots.
Create an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making.
Provide actionable HR recommendations based on the analysis.

## Key Business Questions
Which departments have the highest employee attrition?
Which job roles show higher attrition rates?
Which age groups are more affected by attrition?
How does employee tenure relate to attrition?
Does overtime appear to be associated with higher attrition?
How does business travel relate to employee turnover?
Are early-career employees more likely to leave than experienced employees?
Which employee groups should HR monitor more closely?
What actions can HR take to improve employee retention?

## HR Recommendations

Based on the identified workforce patterns, HR can consider:

Monitoring high-attrition departments and job roles.
Reviewing workload and overtime patterns.
Strengthening support for early-career employees.
Improving employee engagement and satisfaction initiatives.
Reviewing work-life balance concerns.
Conducting regular employee feedback surveys.
Developing targeted retention strategies for identified risk hotspots.
Using workforce analytics regularly to monitor changes in attrition.
