# Workforce-Attrition-Patterns-and-Risk-Hotspot-Analysis-at-Palo-Alto-Networks (created using power bi)
## project objectives
Identify attrition patterns across departments and job roles.
* Analyze attrition based on age, tenure, and career stage.
* Examine the impact of overtime and business travel on employee attrition.
* Identify high-risk workforce segments and attrition hotspots.
* Understand key factors associated with employee turnover.
* Provide data-driven HR recommendations to support employee retention and workforce planning.
